# Artwork

Files: [Wide banner](banner.png) and [square mod-manager cover](thumbnail.png).

Both assets were generated with the built-in image-generation tool and visually reviewed. They match the cream/yellow distressed typography, charcoal texture, angular border and AI disclosure of the other mods. The scene depicts a helmeted Helldiver leaping out of shallow coastal water toward dry ground. PNG ancillary metadata was removed without changing the encoded pixels.

## Banner prompt

Use case: ads-marketing.
Asset type: finished wide 3:1 GitHub mod banner, approximately 2172x724.
Input images: image 1 is a STYLE REFERENCE ONLY from the same mod family, not content to copy.
Primary request: create a polished cover for a Helldivers 2 mod named "Shallow Water Diving".
Style: match the reference's huge heavy condensed distressed cream and military-yellow block typography, charcoal worn graphic texture, thin angular clipped yellow border rules, three yellow hazard slashes, and cinematic realistic game-art illustration.
Text exactly: "Shallow Water" in cream on the first line and "Diving" in yellow beneath, with a small clear footer "AI-assisted development with GPT-6 Astra". No other text.
Composition: title dominates the left roughly 60 percent, with strong readable contrast and generous margins. Right-side scene shows one fully helmeted Helldiver in black/gray armor with yellow accents and a flowing cape, performing a horizontal forward leap from shin-deep water toward a visible dry, low rocky shoreline. Clearly a combat leap to prone, not swimming or scuba diving. Athletic believable anatomy: two arms and two legs, no duplicates.
Background must be meaningfully water-themed: broad shallow gray-teal tidal water, ripples and a small spray at takeoff, submerged pebbles, a muddy low bank and scattered reeds, misty distant alien coastal hills under an overcast sky. The water is visibly shallow, with no huge waves or deep ocean. The dry landing bank is close and reachable. Integrate the scene into the dark banner rather than using a generic volcanic battlefield.
No spacecraft, drop pods, stratagem balls, underwater diver equipment, guns firing, extra people, or unrelated objects. Keep the footer fully inside the border and legible. This is a cohesive original sibling design, not a collage.

## Square cover prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Create a finished square mod-manager cover, approximately 1254x1254, for "Shallow Water Diving".
Input image 1 is the approved wide banner for this mod: preserve its subject, shallow-water coastal setting, armor palette, typography family, border and overall visual identity. Input image 2 is a square-format STYLE AND LAYOUT REFERENCE from another mod; do not reproduce its drop pod or mountainous industrial content.
Recompose as an original matching square cover. Exact title in three large stacked lines at the top: "SHALLOW" cream, "WATER" cream, "DIVING" military yellow. Heavy condensed distressed block lettering on charcoal texture, crisp and readable at thumbnail size.
Lower half: one fully helmeted black/gray-and-yellow armored Helldiver with cape making a horizontal leap out of very shallow gray-teal tidal water toward a nearby dry pebble/muddy bank with a few reeds. Show the reachable landing bank clearly, with small ripples and takeoff splash, shallow submerged stones, hazy alien coast and cloudy sky. This is a leap-to-prone, not swimming or scuba diving. Believable two arms and two legs, no duplicate limbs.
Thin clipped-corner yellow border, three hazard slashes near lower left. Exact small legible footer fully inside the border: "AI-assisted development with GPT-6 Astra". No other words or logos. No spacecraft, drop pods, stratagem balls, underwater gear, giant waves or extra people. Match the approved banner's moody coastal background rather than a generic volcanic battlefield.
